## General

<details>
<summary><strong>What is this extension?</strong></summary>
A browser extension that detects and downloads videos from virtually any website. Supports regular video files, HLS streams, DASH streams, and audio.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is it free?</strong></summary>
Yes — free version available with basic features. <a href="Premium">Premium</a> unlocks unlimited downloads for a one-time $20 payment.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is downloading videos legal?</strong></summary>

Generally legal for personal/offline use. Always respect copyright in your jurisdiction.
- ✅ Personal offline viewing
- ✅ Your own uploads
- ❌ Redistribution of copyrighted content
</details>

## Technical

<details>
<summary><strong>Video not detected?</strong></summary>

1. Play the video first
2. Skip any ads
3. Click "Force detection" in the popup
4. Refresh the page
5. Check if the site uses DRM (cannot be downloaded)
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work with DRM content?</strong></summary>
No. DRM-protected content (Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime, etc.) cannot be downloaded by any browser extension. This is a browser-level restriction.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Max video quality?</strong></summary>

## Privacy

<details>
<summary><strong>Data collection?</strong></summary>
None. No personal data, no tracking, all processing is local, open source.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Required permissions?</strong></summary>

| Permission | Reason |
|------------|--------|
| `webRequest` | Detect video network requests |
| `downloads` | Save files |
| `storage` | Save settings |
| `tabs` | Know current tab |
| `activeTab` | Access page media |
</details>

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary><strong>Corrupted download?</strong></summary>
Try lower quality, check disk space, disable other download managers, try another browser.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Icon doesn't change color?</strong></summary>
Make sure a video is playing. Some sites load video from subdomains — try refreshing.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Slow downloads?</strong></summary>
Speed depends on the source server. Check your connection, close unused tabs.
</details>
